## UAT Studio by Salvion

**User acceptance testing for Salesforce and Microsoft Dynamics 365 go-lives.**

Most UAT still lives in a spreadsheet: nobody knows at the end of the day what passed, what failed and who still has to test. [UAT Studio](https://uat.studio) gives every business tester a clear script, turns a fail into an issue with a screenshot in one click, and tells the project lead at 16:00 whether the go-live can go ahead.

- **A script per tester**: only their own test cases, on a laptop or a phone, in 14 languages
- **Issues with evidence**: screenshots and the test step, sent on to Jira or Azure DevOps
- **Progress live**: per tester, per group and per process, on the dashboard or the big screen
- **Sign-off and go/no-go**: a formal sign-off per group and a report for the steering committee
- **Every release after the go-live**: release readiness for each Salesforce and Dynamics 365 release
